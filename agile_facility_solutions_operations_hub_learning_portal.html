Prime Contractor Governance & NTE Rules

Core operational controls that protect Agile Facility Solutions' profit margins and SLA compliance.

  • Managing Partner Model: Agile holds the client MSA, manages CMMS dispatch, enforces COI compliance, and bills the client at a 25%–35% target gross margin above local vendor buy rates.
  • Not-To-Exceed (NTE) Cap: Every reactive Work Order carries a strict dollar ceiling (e.g., $500–$750 NTE). Vendors must stop work and submit photo proof for a written NTE increase if repairs exceed the cap.
  • IVR / CMMS Check-In & Out: Technicians must log arrival/departure timestamps and upload clear, timestamped Before and After photos before invoice approval.
  • 2-to-3 Bid Rule: For capital projects or recurring contracts over $2,500/month, Vendor Coordinators obtain at least 2–3 competitive local subcontractor bids before locking client pricing.
